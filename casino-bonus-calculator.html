Bonus math guide

How this bonus reality check works

How bonus amount is calculated

The tool calculates bonus amount as the lesser of deposit × bonus match percentage and the max bonus cap.

Why wagering requirements matter

Wagering requirements multiply what must be bet before terms clear. Deposit + bonus terms can double the base and magnify expected loss.

Why RTP matters

House edge is calculated as 1 − RTP. That edge is applied to adjusted wagering to estimate long-run expected loss.

Why game contribution matters

Lower contribution means more real wagering to satisfy the same requirement. A 50% contribution doubles the adjusted wagering load.

How max cashout is treated

This calculator treats max cashout as a total withdrawal cap from bonus play. Profit above the original deposit is capped.

Common bonus traps

  • High wagering requirements
  • Low game contribution
  • Max bet limits
  • Game exclusions
  • Short time limits

Casino bonus FAQ

What does this calculator estimate?

It estimates theoretical bonus value after wagering requirements, game contribution, RTP house edge, optional cashout caps, and optional fees.

How is max cashout handled?

Max cashout is treated as the maximum total withdrawal from bonus play. Profit above your original deposit is capped at max cashout minus deposit.

Does this predict what happens tonight?

No. This tool gives long-run math estimates. Short-term outcomes can be very different because variance and bonus rules still apply.
