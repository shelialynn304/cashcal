Interpretation

Read the result like a risk report

Bet size drives bust risk

Small RTP differences matter over time, but oversized spins can end a session before the long-run average has any room to show up.

Volatility widens the range

High volatility creates wider outcome ranges around the same average return, which means longer droughts and sharper drawdowns.

One win is not enough

A single big hit can save one session, but it does not change the steady edge against your bankroll over many spins.

Questions players ask

Slot Bankroll Survival FAQ

Short answers about ruin risk, target spins, bankroll pressure, and why this calculator is a model instead of a prediction.

What does slot bankroll survival mean?

Slot bankroll survival estimates whether a bankroll can last through a target number of spins based on bet size, RTP, volatility, and simulation assumptions.

What is ruin risk in a slot session?

Ruin risk is the estimated chance that the bankroll hits zero before reaching the target number of spins in the modeled session.

Does this calculator predict real slot results?

No. It models bankroll pressure and simulated outcomes, but it cannot predict results from any specific casino machine or future spin.

How can I make a slot bankroll last longer?

Lowering bet size, reducing target spins, and avoiding high-volatility profiles can usually reduce bankroll pressure, but none of those choices removes the house edge.
