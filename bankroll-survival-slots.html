Ruin risk is the model's true simulated bust probability from the session Monte Carlo.

Why this matters Ruin risk tells you how often the bankroll hit zero before the target spins, while average RTP measures the long-run expected loss rate. You can still survive a session with a negative average RTP, but a higher ruin risk means your bankroll is more likely to run out during one normal slot session.

Session RTP is a long-run average, not a guarantee for a single session. Focus on ruin risk if you want a safer bankroll path.

Interpretation

Read the result like a risk report

Bet size drives bust risk

Small RTP differences matter over time, but oversized spins can end a session before the long-run average has any room to show up.

Volatility widens the range

High volatility creates wider outcome ranges around the same average return, which means longer droughts and sharper drawdowns.

One win is not enough

A single big hit can save one session, but it does not change the steady edge against your bankroll over many spins.
