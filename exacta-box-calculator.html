Exacta box basics

An exacta box pays if any two of your horses finish first and second, in any order. With 4 horses you cover 12 combinations. With 5 horses that rises to 20.

More combinations cost more. The box does not change the pool takeout or the odds offered by the track.

What this calculator does not do

This is a ticket cost calculator, not a payout predictor. Exacta payouts are pari-mutuel: the final pool, track takeout rate, and number of winning tickets determine what a winning exacta pays.

A lower-cost box does not mean a better return. Payout depends on pool size and competition, not ticket count.

How to calculate exacta box cost

  1. Enter the dollar amount you want to bet per combination (e.g., $1 or $2).
  2. Enter how many horses are in your exacta box. Minimum is 2.
  3. The calculator applies n × (n − 1) to count finishing-order combinations.
  4. Ticket cost = combinations × base bet. For 4 horses at $1: 12 × $1 = $12.
  5. Compare that cost to your bankroll before buying the ticket.

Coverage is not the same as value

Adding horses to an exacta box increases coverage but also raises cost at an accelerating rate. A 4-horse box costs $12 at $1 base. A 6-horse box costs $30.

More horses do not automatically create better expected value. Exotics are high-variance bets and the pool takeout applies regardless of how many combinations you buy.

Exacta Box FAQ

How much does a $1 exacta box cost?

A $1 exacta box costs $6 with 3 horses, $12 with 4 horses, and $20 with 5 horses. An exacta box has n × (n − 1) combinations, and each one costs $1.

How much does a $2 exacta box cost?

A $2 exacta box costs $12 with 3 horses and $24 with 4 horses. That is 6 and 12 combinations, each at $2.

How much does a $5 exacta box cost?

A $5 exacta box costs $30 with 3 horses and $60 with 4 horses. The combination count is the same at every base bet; only the price per combination changes.

How much is a 4 horse exacta box?

A 4 horse exacta box is 12 combinations (4 × 3 = 12), so it costs $12 at a $1 base and $24 at a $2 base.

How much is a $1 exacta box with 5 horses?

A $1 exacta box with 5 horses costs $20, because 5 horses create 20 combinations (5 × 4 = 20) at $1 each.

What is an exacta box bet?

An exacta box is a bet on two or more horses to finish first and second in either order; a $1 box on 3 horses is 6 combinations, so it costs $6. Boxing more horses buys more coverage, but only one combination can win, and it is paid at your base bet from the pari-mutuel pool.

How is an exacta box different from a straight exacta?

A straight exacta requires one specific finishing order — horse A first, horse B second. An exacta box covers every order among your selected horses, so it hits whenever two of your horses finish first and second, in either order. The box costs more because it buys multiple combinations.

How does pari-mutuel takeout affect exacta payouts?

Track takeout is deducted from the total exacta pool before payouts, and the rest is split among winning tickets in proportion to the amount bet on the winning combination. Your box money joins the pool, but only the one winning combination is paid, at your base bet. Adding horses raises cost and coverage, not the payout on a hit.
