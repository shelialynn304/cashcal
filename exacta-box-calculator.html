Exacta box basics

An exacta box pays if any two of your horses finish first and second, in any order. With 4 horses you cover 12 combinations. With 5 horses that rises to 20.

More combinations cost more. The box does not change the pool takeout or the odds offered by the track.

Exacta box cost calculator

Enter your base bet per combination and the number of horses in the box. The calculator applies n × (n − 1) to find total combinations, then multiplies by your base bet.

Enter a base bet and horse count, then press Calculate to see ticket combinations and estimated cost.

What this calculator does not do

This is a ticket cost calculator, not a payout predictor. Exacta payouts are pari-mutuel: the final pool, track takeout rate, and number of winning tickets determine what a winning exacta pays.

A lower-cost box does not mean a better return. Payout depends on pool size and competition, not ticket count.

How to calculate exacta box cost

  1. Enter the dollar amount you want to bet per combination (e.g., $1 or $2).
  2. Enter how many horses are in your exacta box. Minimum is 2.
  3. The calculator applies n × (n − 1) to count finishing-order combinations.
  4. Ticket cost = combinations × base bet. For 4 horses at $1: 12 × $1 = $12.
  5. Compare that cost to your bankroll before buying the ticket.

Coverage is not the same as value

Adding horses to an exacta box increases coverage but also raises cost at an accelerating rate. A 4-horse box costs $12 at $1 base. A 6-horse box costs $30.

More horses do not automatically create better expected value. Exotics are high-variance bets and the pool takeout applies regardless of how many combinations you buy.

Exacta Box FAQ

What is the minimum exacta box?

The minimum exacta box uses 2 horses, creating 2 combinations (2 × 1 = 2). At a $1 base, the ticket costs $2.

How is an exacta box different from a straight exacta?

A straight exacta requires one specific finishing order — horse A first, horse B second. An exacta box covers every order among your selected horses, so it hits regardless of which of your horses finishes first or second. The box costs more because it buys multiple combinations.

Does a larger exacta box increase my chances of winning?

A larger box increases coverage, meaning more finishing-order combinations are covered. It does not improve the underlying edge or change the track takeout rate. You are paying more for broader coverage, not for a better mathematical return.

How does pari-mutuel takeout affect exacta payouts?

Track takeout is deducted from the total exacta pool before any payouts are calculated. The remaining pool splits among all winning tickets. A higher-cost box does not affect the takeout percentage or the size of the pool — it only determines how much of the pool you have claim to if you win.

Can I use this calculator for exacta keys or partial boxes?

No. This calculator covers full box bets only, where every selected horse can finish in any covered position. Exacta keys and partial boxes use different combination structures and require a separate calculation.
