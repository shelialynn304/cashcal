Responsible Gambling

Use gambling math to understand risk — not to chase losses.

EdgeOverLuck.com is for adults 21 and older. Our calculators and simulators are educational tools that show risk, variance, and expected value; they do not make gambling safe or profitable.

Play with the numbers before you play with your money.

Core Safety Rules

Set a fixed entertainment budget, decide stop-loss and time limits before playing, never borrow money to gamble, and never gamble while impaired or under pressure.

Know the Warning Signs

Take a break and seek help if you hide gambling, chase losses, miss bills, gamble with money needed for essentials, or feel unable to stop.

Help Resources

In the United States, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER for confidential support. You can also visit ncpgambling.org for additional responsible gambling information.

How Our Tools Should Be Used

Use the bankroll calculator, blackjack trainer, and slot simulator to understand downside risk and make more informed decisions. Do not use any tool as a reason to bet more than you can afford.
